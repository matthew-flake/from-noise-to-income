Test photos for the projects that start from your own image. They were generated by the author with
Z-Image-Turbo (Apache 2.0) and are free to use for practice.

- product.png   - an unbranded skincare jar, used by P08 (product placement) and P15 (six studio looks)
- landscape.png - a mountain-lake landscape, used by P13 (21:9 panorama)

Copy them into ComfyUI/input, or pick your own photos in the Load Image node.
`scripts/make_release.py` copies them here from ComfyUI's output/book/_inputs folder (metadata removed).
